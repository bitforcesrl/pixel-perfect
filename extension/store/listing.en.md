## Description

Your site on top of its Figma design. See every pixel that doesn't match.

Pixel Perfect (https://pixelperfect.fit) is a free, open-source design QA tool. It stacks the live site on its Figma design, per page and per viewport, and finds the drift in seconds:

• Split — slide a seam across both layers: whatever does not line up breaks at the seam.
• Overlay — difference blending turns everything that matches black, and whatever drifts glows.
• Tap — hold to flip from the build to the design.
• Side by side — two columns, one scroll, for long pages.

Guides, a pen, notes and a diff map turn what you find into a ticket, and a whole review travels as one ZIP. No account: your projects stay in your browser.

Pixel Perfect Capture brings the live site in. It takes the screenshots in your own browser:

• Your session, your state — if you are logged in, past a store password and done with the cookie banner, so is the capture. No accounts, no API keys, no cloud browser.
• Every viewport — mobile, tablet, desktop or your own sizes, with a real phone layout below 768 px and the same pixel density as the Figma export.
• Full page — lazy images loaded, smooth-scroll wrappers unwrapped, very tall pages stitched.
• Clean — cookie banners, newsletter pop-ups and the Shopify preview bar are hidden from the screenshot, never clicked: your choices on the site stay as they were.
• One click from any site — the toolbar button opens the page in Pixel Perfect (or creates it) and captures it.

How it works: Pixel Perfect asks for a capture, you allow the site once, a small window opens the page at each viewport, takes the screenshots and closes. Chrome shows a "started debugging this browser" bar while it works: that is how an extension can show a page at a phone size.

Privacy: nothing is collected or sent anywhere. Screenshots go only to your Pixel Perfect tab, in your browser. The source code is public: https://github.com/bitforcesrl/pixel-perfect

Works with pixelperfect.fit, with Pixel Perfect running on localhost, and with a self-hosted copy you add in the settings.
